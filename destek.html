FAQ

Did I lose my progress? The game saves your progress on your device automatically. Tap "Devam Et" (Continue) on the main menu to resume. Deleting the app deletes the save.

My score isn't on the leaderboard. Make sure you're signed in under iOS Settings → Game Center. Scores are sent when a career or season ends; if you're offline, the game retries next time it opens.

The radio is silent. Check that your phone isn't in silent mode and the volume is up.
